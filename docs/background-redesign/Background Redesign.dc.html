Circuit Break · background redesign

Depth by value, not by detail.

Same world, same tokens, same five-layer contract. The far ground is painted in the sky's own colours at partial alpha, the mid ground in night, the near ground in abyss/void, and every layer dissolves into fog at its base. Silhouettes get roof profiles from one seeded layout that both the body layer and its lights layer read.

What's wrong today
Every zone is the same blocky-rect skyline with a palette swap. One depth cue (size). Hard bases on every layer. Zones don't escalate, they recolour.
The move
Three value tiers (haze → night → void), fog bands, drawn roof profiles, one accent hue per zone that the mood grade can push. Zero new tokens, zero new assets, same texture budget.
01 · Office park — ordinary
Horizontal and hushed. Low campus blocks lit by whole floors (a cleaning crew, not a party), sodium lamp pools, hedges and cars in near-black, two ridges of hills with a treeline. Accent: warning. Motion: a slow breathing of the lit floors.
02 · Neon city — electric
Vertical and dense. Three depths of towers with setbacks, slants and spires; signs, crowns and spire-tips in cyan/magenta/lime that bleed onto the street; an elevated overpass with a lamp string in the foreground. Accent: the three neons. Motion: signage pulses.
03 · Stadium — arena
The race is inside the bowl: one continuous grandstand that tiles seamlessly — canopy, two raked tiers, a crowd of thousands, vomitories, a gold LED ribbon, flood masts fanning light down. Accent: gold + silver. Motion: camera flashes, masts sweep.

Deliverables

lib/world/content/nightRace.ts — drop-in replacement. Same exports, same WorldDefinition shape, 5 layers per zone / 2 core, ambient only on rich layers, all colours from COLOR. Every mockup above is this exact file drawn through a Canvas2D stand-in for Pixi Graphics (mock/render.js) and composited with ParallaxLayer.update's math.
Token additions: none. Atmosphere comes from alpha over existing tokens; the one derived colour (mix() inside gradient bands) already existed in the current file. tests/tokens.test.ts and the a11y palette are untouched.
Tests: tests/worldDefinition.test.ts stays green unchanged — no count or ordering rule needed to move.
Texture budget: sky tiles drop from 1600×900 to 1024×900 (the gradient is horizontally uniform; stars repeat at 1024 world units without a visible seam). Per-zone tile pixels: office 2.4M, city 3.7M, stadium 3.2M — at or under the current 4.1M each.

Optional second step

Sky seam at wide spans (fixed for free here). At the 14-segment span a 900-unit sky is 387px tall on a 1080 canvas, so the clear colour showed above it. Every sky now opens in abyss — the canvas clear — so the join is invisible. No renderer change.
Grade.ts. The neutral grade tints with void, which flattens the new value separation as the game progresses. Consider night at the same alphas for the neutral hue so the vignette darkens without desaturating, and let the final-question hue key off the zone: neonMagenta in the city, gold at a lower peak (≈0.24) in the stadium, so the arena grades hot instead of pink.
zones.ts. Widen ZONE_OVERLAP from 0.12 to 0.18 — with fog bands on both sides a longer crossfade reads as weather rolling in rather than a dissolve. Boundaries and the reduced-profile hard switch stay as they are.
body gradient. Unchanged. The page's dusk top-glow and 14% magenta corner already sit between the office and city skies; the stadium's silver light dome is inside the canvas only, which is where it should be.
